SCIENCE EXPO 2026 — DRAFT EXHIBITOR GUIDELINES
EGS Pillay Group of Institutions

STATUS: Sample planning guidance. Not final event terms.
Proposed event: 24 October 2026, EGS Pillay Campus, Nagapattinam.

ELIGIBILITY
School, diploma, undergraduate and postgraduate student teams.
Proposed team size: 2–4 students with a faculty mentor.
One main project per team. Original work with sources acknowledged.

TRACKS
Space, Satellite, Racing, Aero, Robotics, Physics, Mathematics, Engineering.

SUBMISSION CHECKLIST
- 200–300 word abstract: problem, solution, development stage.
- Team members, institution, faculty mentor and team contact.
- Image or diagram of the proposed exhibit.
- Demonstration plan and evidence of testing where available.
- Space, power, connectivity and safety requirements.

PROPOSED DATES
Project submissions: 01–10 October 2026.
Selection notification: 15 October 2026.
Exhibit setup: 23 October 2026.
Expo day: 24 October 2026.

SAFETY
Declare batteries, moving parts, chemicals, heat and specialist equipment.
Obtain organiser approval before running a live demonstration.
Keep a team member at the exhibit during opening hours.

JUDGING
Scientific understanding, originality, execution, testing and communication.
Clearly separate measured findings from proposed outcomes.

REGISTRATION
Registration is OFFLINE ONLY at the designated campus registration desk.
Bring student/institution ID, a printed abstract, team and faculty mentor details.
Visit /help-desk/registration for the current desk location and hours.
Fees, certificates and final terms are to be confirmed by the organisers.
There is no online registration form, registration link or online payment.
